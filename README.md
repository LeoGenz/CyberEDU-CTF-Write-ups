# 🚩 CyberEDU CTF Write-ups

Welcome! This repository is a personal collection of my write-ups and solutions for various Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges from the [CyberEDU](https://cyberedu.ro/) platform. 

I am currently a beginner in cybersecurity, so I created this repo to document my learning journey, keep track of the tools I use, and share my thought process as I figure things out.

## 🎯 Why I Made This
* **To Learn:** Writing down how I solved a challenge helps me understand the concepts better.
* **Future Reference:** A place I can look back on when I forget a specific command, tool, or technique.
* **Share with Others:** To help out fellow beginners who might be stuck on the same challenges.

## 📂 Categories Covered
As I explore the platform, you'll find basic write-ups across different categories:
* 🌐 **Web Exploitation**
* 🕵️ **Forensics**
* 🔐 **Cryptography**
* 🐚 **Pwn / Binary Exploitation**
* 🧩 **Reverse Engineering**
* 🎲 **Misc (Miscellaneous)**

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
1. **Spoilers Ahead!** These folders contain full solutions and flags. If you are actively trying to solve a challenge on CyberEDU, I highly recommend trying it on your own first before reading these!
2. **Beginner Alert:** Since I am still learning and not an advanced player, my solutions might not always be the cleanest, fastest, or most "intended" way to solve a problem—they are simply what worked for me at the time. 

## 🤝 Feedback & Contributions
If you are reading through my write-ups and know a better tool, a cooler trick, or a simpler way to solve a challenge, feel free to open an issue or reach out! I am always open to learning new things.
